Mulund West, Mumbai - The Prince of Suburbs

Mulund West is one of Mumbai's most loved residential suburbs - a clean, green and well-planned neighbourhood nestled at the foot of the Yeoor Hills and Sanjay Gandhi National Park. Character & Vibe

What Makes Mulund West Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Mulund West runs on a calm, family-first rhythm. Mornings see joggers heading to the hills and gardens; days hum with markets on Zaver Road and shoppers at the malls; and evenings are for community gatherings, garba grounds and quiet society life. Clean roads, greenery and a strong civic sense set it apart from busier Mumbai suburbs.

The community is predominantly Gujarati and Jain, with strong Maharashtrian and South Indian populations too - vegetarian, traditional yet modern, and deeply community-minded. That blend of greenery, safety and warmth is exactly why it's called the Prince of Suburbs.
