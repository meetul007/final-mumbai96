About the Area

Bhandup, Mumbai - Industrial Roots, Residential Rise

Bhandup is a vibrant mixed-use suburb in central-east Mumbai, balancing its old industrial estates with a fast-rising residential scene. Bordered by the Thane creek wetlands and the Eastern Express Highway, it offers value housing, a busy market culture and the same green eastern-suburb feel - just minutes from Powai and Mulund.
