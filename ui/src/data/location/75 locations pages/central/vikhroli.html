About the Area

Vikhroli, Mumbai - The House of Godrej

Vikhroli is a one-of-a-kind central-east Mumbai suburb defined by the Godrej Group - home to the sprawling Pirojshanagar township, the historic Godrej & Boyce manufacturing complex, the Godrej One corporate HQ and the city's largest privately-protected mangrove forest. It blends corporate energy, industrial heritage, value housing and rare urban greenery.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Vikhroli Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Vikhroli lives a double life shaped by Godrej. The west side hums with corporate offices, green campuses and the calm of the mangroves; the east buzzes with dense colonies like Kannamwar Nagar and Tagore Nagar, busy markets and everyday family life. Few Mumbai suburbs pair big-company polish with such grounded, community-driven living.

The community is a friendly mix of Maharashtrian, South Indian, North Indian and Gujarati families - many with multi-generational ties to Godrej - alongside young professionals drawn by value housing and Powai proximity. It's hardworking, rooted and quietly green.
