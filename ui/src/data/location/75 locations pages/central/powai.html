About the Area

Powai, Mumbai - Lakeside Living & IT Hub

Powai is one of Mumbai's most aspirational planned neighbourhoods - built around the scenic Powai Lake and IIT Bombay, and anchored by the landmark Hiranandani Gardens township. Character & Vibe

What Makes Powai Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Powai has a polished, almost self-contained rhythm. Mornings bring lakeside joggers and IIT cyclists; days hum with the IT parks and the buzz of the Galleria; and evenings glow as Hiranandani Gardens' bars and cafes fill with a young, well-travelled crowd. Few Mumbai neighbourhoods feel this planned, green and globally connected.

The community is highly cosmopolitan - IT and corporate professionals, IIT academics and students, entrepreneurs, expats and affluent families. It's aspirational, fast-paced and modern, with a lifestyle that often feels a world apart from the rest of the city.
