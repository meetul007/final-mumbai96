Character & Vibe

What Makes Breach Candy Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Breach Candy is South Mumbai at its most serene. The streets are wide and tree-lined. The buildings are set back from the road. The sea is never more than a five-minute walk away. And the Breach Candy Club - founded in 1884 - gives the neighbourhood a social anchor that is unlike anything else in Mumbai. This is a place that has always known exactly what it is.

The residents are a fascinating mix of old Bombay families - Parsis, Gujaratis, Sindhis - who have lived here for generations, and a newer wave of professionals and executives drawn by the address, the schools and the hospitals. Everyone here values the same things: quiet, greenery, the sea and the knowledge that two of India's finest hospitals are within walking distance. Breach Candy is not a neighbourhood you move to. It is a neighbourhood you arrive at.
