About the Area

Malabar Hill, Mumbai - Mumbai's Most Elevated & Prestigious Residential Neighbourhood

Malabar Hill is where Mumbai looks down on itself - literally and figuratively. Perched on a promontory overlooking the Arabian Sea and Marine Drive, it is the city's most elevated and prestigious residential neighbourhood. Home to the Hanging Gardens, the ancient Banganga Tank, the Walkeshwar Temple, the Maharashtra Chief Minister's official residence and some of the most expensive real estate in India, it is a neighbourhood of extraordinary natural beauty, deep spiritual heritage and absolute social prestige.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Malabar Hill Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Malabar Hill is Mumbai at its most elevated - in every sense. The streets are quiet. The air is cleaner. The views are extraordinary. And the residents move through their days with the unhurried confidence of people who have always lived at the top of the city. This is not a neighbourhood that announces itself. It simply is.

The morning walkers at the Hanging Gardens, the devotees at the Banganga Tank, the children at the Kamala Nehru Park, the politicians at Varsha - they all share the same hill, the same sea breeze and the same extraordinary views. Malabar Hill is the only place in Mumbai where you can watch the Queen's Necklace from above, where the ancient and the ultra-modern coexist without friction, and where the city's most powerful people live alongside its most sacred sites.

Who Lives in Malabar Hill?

Mumbai's most distinguished residents - here's who you'll find calling Malabar Hill home.

Malabar Hill is home to some of Mumbai's - and India's - most powerful and distinguished families. The Maharashtra Chief Minister lives at Varsha on B.G. Kher Marg. Senior politicians, judges of the Bombay High Court, industrialists and old Parsi families have called this hill home for generations. The Hanging Gardens are their morning walk. The Banganga Tank is their spiritual anchor. And the view of Marine Drive from their windows is their daily reminder of why there is nowhere else in Mumbai quite like this.
