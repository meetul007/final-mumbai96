About the Area

Cuffe Parade, Mumbai - Maker Towers, World Trade Centre, Badhwar Park & Mumbai's Most Exclusive Sea-Facing Address

Cuffe Parade is Mumbai's southernmost residential enclave - a narrow peninsula of reclaimed land jutting into the Arabian Sea at the very tip of South Mumbai. Created from land reclaimed from the sea in the 1970s as part of the Back Bay Reclamation Scheme, it is today one of the most exclusive and expensive residential addresses in India. The iconic Maker Towers, the World Trade Centre, Badhwar Park's bungalows and the sea-facing towers along Cuffe Parade Main Road define an area that is simultaneously a diplomatic quarter, a corporate address and a supremely quiet residential neighbourhood.

Cuffe Parade, Mumbai - The Complete Guide

Land Reclaimed, Legends Created

Cuffe Parade did not exist before the 1970s. It was sea. The Back Bay Reclamation Scheme - one of independent India's most ambitious urban engineering projects - created this narrow peninsula of land at the southernmost tip of South Mumbai, and within a decade it had become one of the most coveted addresses in the country. The Maker Towers complex - eight interconnected residential towers with sea views on three sides - was completed in the early 1980s and immediately became the defining landmark of the new neighbourhood. The World Trade Centre followed, bringing diplomatic missions, trade offices and international organisations to what had been open water just years before. Today, Cuffe Parade's pin code 400005 is synonymous with exclusivity, sea views and the quiet confidence of old money.

The Maker Towers Community

Maker Towers is not just a residential complex - it is a community. The eight towers share a residents' club with a swimming pool, gymnasium and recreational facilities. The building committees are active and well-organised. The security is tight. And the residents - a mix of retired industrialists, senior executives, NRI families and diplomats - have created a self-contained, self-sufficient neighbourhood within a neighbourhood. A flat in Maker Towers is not just a home; it is membership in one of Mumbai's most exclusive clubs. Sea-facing units command a significant premium over non-sea-facing ones, and the waiting list for available flats is long. Most transactions happen through word-of-mouth and trusted agents, never on public platforms.

Badhwar Park - Mumbai's Most Discreet Address

If Maker Towers is Cuffe Parade's most famous address, Badhwar Park is its most discreet. This quiet enclave of bungalows and low-rise buildings - tucked away from the main road, shaded by old trees - is home to retired IAS and IPS officers, senior diplomats and old-money Mumbai families who have lived here since the reclamation was completed. There are no shops in Badhwar Park, no restaurants, no commercial activity of any kind. Just wide, quiet lanes, well-maintained bungalows and the sound of the sea. It is, by any measure, one of the most peaceful and prestigious addresses in India.

Metro Line 3 - The Game Changer

For decades, Cuffe Parade's only weakness was connectivity. The peninsula's geography meant that getting to BKC, the airport or the western suburbs required a long drive through South Mumbai's congested streets. Metro Line 3 changed everything. The Cuffe Parade station - the southernmost on the entire line - now connects the peninsula to BKC in 25 minutes, the airport in 40 minutes and Andheri in under an hour. For Cuffe Parade's executive community, this was transformative. The area's already-high property values rose further.
