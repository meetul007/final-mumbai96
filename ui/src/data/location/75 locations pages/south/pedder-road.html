About the Area

Pedder Road, Mumbai - Mumbai's Most Prestigious Address & Old Money Heartland

Pedder Road is where Mumbai's elite have always lived. Flanked by Kemps Corner, Breach Candy, Cumballa Hill and Napeansea Road, it is a neighbourhood of heritage bungalows, luxury high-rises, manicured gardens and a quiet, unhurried elegance that no other part of Mumbai can replicate. This is old money, old families and old Bombay at its most refined.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Pedder Road Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Pedder Road does not announce itself. There are no neon signs, no crowded markets, no honking auto-rickshaws. The streets are wide and tree-lined. The buildings are set back from the road. The residents move quietly and purposefully. This is a neighbourhood that has always known exactly what it is - and has never needed to prove it to anyone.

Old Parsi families, Gujarati industrialists, Sindhi business dynasties and a new generation of tech billionaires all call Pedder Road home. The Breach Candy Club is the social anchor. Jaslok and Breach Candy hospitals are the healthcare anchors. Campion and Cathedral are the school anchors. Everything here is the best of its kind - and the residents expect nothing less.
