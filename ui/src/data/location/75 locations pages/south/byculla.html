About the Area

Byculla, Mumbai - Mumbai's Oldest Zoo, Multi-Community Heritage & A Neighbourhood Reborn

Byculla is one of Mumbai's oldest and most historically layered localities. Home to the Byculla Zoo - Mumbai's oldest - the Mazgaon docks, the Jewish Sassoon family's legacy, grand old churches, mosques and temples, it is a neighbourhood where every lane tells a story. Today, Byculla is also one of Mumbai's most exciting real estate transformation stories, with luxury towers rising alongside century-old heritage buildings.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Byculla Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Byculla is Mumbai's most quietly extraordinary neighbourhood. Walk its lanes and you'll pass a 400-year-old church, a magnificent synagogue, a mosque with a century of history, and a Hindu temple - all within a few minutes of each other. This is not a curated heritage walk. This is just Tuesday in Byculla.

The neighbourhood is in the middle of a fascinating transformation. Old chawls and heritage buildings stand next to gleaming luxury towers. The Bohri Muslim community of Nagpada, the East Indian Catholics of Mazgaon, the Maharashtrian families of Chinchpokli and the new-age professionals moving into the luxury developments - they all share the same streets, the same markets and the same deep attachment to a place that refuses to be ordinary.

One of Mumbai's most diverse communities - here's who you'll find calling Byculla home.

Central Railway - Byculla & Chinchpokli Stations

Two Central Railway stations serve Byculla - Byculla Station (10 min to CST) and Chinchpokli Station. Fast and slow trains connect to Dadar, Thane and beyond.

Eastern Freeway - Byculla to BKC & Beyond

The Eastern Freeway entry near Mazgaon connects Byculla to BKC in 15 minutes and Chembur in 20 - a game-changer for road commuters heading east.

BEST Bus Routes from Byculla

Key routes: 1, 3 (Colaba–Bandra), 63 (Byculla–Dadar), 66 (Nagpada–CST), 83 (Byculla–Nana Chowk), 124 (Byculla–Churchgate). Covers all internal pockets well.

Jacob Circle Road Access

Jacob Circle connects Byculla to Mahalaxmi, Worli and the Bandra-Worli Sea Link - ideal for western suburb commuters by road, cutting travel time significantly.

Events & Festivals

Local Events in Byculla

From Ramzan nights in Nagpada to Christmas at Gloria Church and Ganesh Chaturthi processions - Byculla's festival calendar is the most diverse in Mumbai.

Mar – Apr

Ramzan & Eid in Nagpada, Byculla

Nagpada transforms during Ramzan - iftar stalls, late-night food markets, qawwali nights and Eid celebrations that draw visitors from across Mumbai. One of the city's most atmospheric festival experiences.

Aug – Sep

Ganesh Chaturthi in Byculla

Byculla's mandals celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi with elaborate decorations and processions through the historic lanes - a deeply felt neighbourhood tradition.

Dec

Christmas at Gloria Church, Byculla

The historic Gloria Church hosts midnight mass and Christmas celebrations that draw the East Indian Catholic community from across Mumbai - a beautiful and moving tradition.

Oct

Navratri Garba in Byculla

Byculla's Gujarati and Maharashtrian communities celebrate Navratri with garba nights in open grounds - a lively annual tradition in the neighbourhood.

Year Round

Byculla Zoo Family Events

The Byculla Zoo hosts seasonal events, school visits and conservation awareness programmes - a beloved family destination that draws visitors from across Mumbai year-round.

Year Round
