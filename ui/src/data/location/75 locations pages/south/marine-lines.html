About the Area

Marine Lines, Mumbai - Queen's Necklace, Marine Drive & South Mumbai's Finest Address

Marine Lines is where Mumbai is at its most beautiful. Home to the iconic Marine Drive - the Queen's Necklace - it is one of the world's great urban seafronts. The area combines the prestige of Nariman Point, the heritage of Churchgate, the greenery of Azad Maidan and the quiet elegance of its residential lanes into one of India's most coveted addresses.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Marine Lines Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Marine Lines operates at two speeds. By day, it is all business - Churchgate's commuter rush, Nariman Point's corporate corridors, lawyers in black coats striding to the High Court. By evening, it transforms into something else entirely: the promenade fills with walkers, joggers, couples and families, all drawn to the same thing - the sea, the breeze and the lights.

It is one of the few places in Mumbai where you can walk from your front door to the Arabian Sea in under two minutes. The residents know this and guard it fiercely. Old Parsi families, senior advocates, corporate executives and a growing number of young professionals who have discovered that no suburb can replicate what Marine Lines offers - the city at its most civilised.

Just 3 minutes from Churchgate terminus - the fastest South Mumbai commute on the Western Line. Established in 1867, compact and walkable to Marine Drive.

The Colaba-Bandra-SEEPZ underground metro has a station near Churchgate - transforming east-west connectivity for Marine Lines residents to BKC and beyond.

BEST Bus Routes from Marine Lines

Key routes: 1, 2, 3 (Colaba–Bandra), 106 (Marine Lines–Dadar), 132 (Marine Lines–Kurla). Covers all internal pockets and connects to the suburbs efficiently.

The Eastern Freeway connects Marine Lines to Chembur and the eastern suburbs in under 20 minutes. The Bandra-Worli Sea Link is accessible via Marine Drive for western suburb commuters.
