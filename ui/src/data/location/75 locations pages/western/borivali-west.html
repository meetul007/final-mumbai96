About the Area

Borivali West, Mumbai - Green, Residential & Well-Connected

Borivali West is one of Mumbai's most livable and self-sufficient suburbs, sitting at the northern edge of the Western Suburbs. It is the gateway to Sanjay Gandhi National Park - the world's largest urban national park - and home to a thriving Gujarati community, well-planned residential colonies like IC Colony, and a bustling commercial belt along Chandavarkar Road and S.V. Road.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Borivali West Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Borivali West is Mumbai's green lung suburb. It wakes up early - morning walkers head to SGNP's gates by 6am, the station gets busy by 7:30am, and Chandavarkar Road hums with shoppers and office-goers by 9am. By evening, IC Colony's wide lanes fill with families on evening walks, and the food stalls near the station do brisk business.

It's overwhelmingly Gujarati in character - farsan shops, Jain restaurants, garba grounds and community halls define the social fabric. But it's also home to Maharashtrian families, South Indian communities and a growing number of young professionals drawn by the greenery, connectivity and relative affordability compared to Andheri or Bandra.

Resident Profile

Who Lives in Borivali West?

A cosmopolitan cross-section of Mumbai - here's who you'll find calling Borivali West home.

Borivali West is quintessentially a family suburb. IC Colony's bungalows and societies are home to multi-generational Gujarati families who have lived here for decades. Shimpoli and Eksar attract young couples and nuclear families drawn by newer housing projects. Chandavarkar Road's commercial belt brings in traders and small business owners, while the proximity to SGNP draws nature lovers and health-conscious residents who value the green environment.
