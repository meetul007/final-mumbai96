About the Area

Dahisar West, Mumbai - The City's Northern Gateway Suburb

Dahisar West is the northernmost suburb within Mumbai city limits, falling under the BMC's R/North ward. Known for its affordable housing, busy LT Road and SVP Road markets, and proximity to Sanjay Gandhi National Park, it offers genuine Mumbai-city living at a fraction of central-suburb prices.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Dahisar West Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Dahisar West is a classic Mumbai middle-class suburb. Mornings begin with the rush to the station and metro, the LT Road market hums through the day, and evenings bring families out for street food and walks. It's value-driven Mumbai-city living - genuine 400-pin-code Mumbai at far gentler prices than the southern suburbs.

The community is a steady mix of Gujarati, Marathi and North Indian families, many settled here for decades. With Sanjay Gandhi National Park on its doorstep and excellent new Metro links, Dahisar West blends old-suburb warmth with a fast-improving quality of life.
