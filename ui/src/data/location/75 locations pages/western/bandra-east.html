Bandra East, Mumbai - BKC Business District & Civic Hub

Bandra East is one of Mumbai's most important commercial corridors, anchored by the Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC) - India's premier planned business district. What Makes Bandra East Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Bandra East lives a double life. By day, BKC roars - suited bankers, glass towers, valet queues and power lunches.
