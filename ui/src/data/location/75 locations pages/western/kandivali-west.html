About the Area

Kandivali West, Mumbai - Townships, Families & Self-Sufficient Living

Kandivali West is one of Mumbai's most complete residential suburbs - a place where large planned townships like Thakur Village and Lokhandwala coexist with the government-planned Charkop colony, the Poisar river belt and a thriving commercial strip along S.V. Road and Akurli Road. It is a suburb that has everything within reach - schools, hospitals, malls, markets and excellent Western Railway connectivity.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Kandivali West Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Kandivali West is the definition of a complete suburb. Thakur Village alone functions like a small city - it has its own schools, hospitals, markets, parks and restaurants. Lokhandwala Township adds an upscale layer, while Charkop brings a grounded, government-colony character. The result is a suburb that works for everyone - from young professionals in Lokhandwala to retired government employees in Charkop to growing families in Thakur Village.

It's cosmopolitan but with a strong Gujarati and Maharashtrian backbone. The Navratri garba in Thakur Village is legendary - one of the best in Mumbai. The Ganesh festival in Charkop is equally grand. Metro 2A has already transformed daily commutes, and the suburb continues to attract new residents priced out of Borivali and Andheri.

Resident Profile

Who Lives in Kandivali West?

A diverse and well-settled cross-section of Mumbai - here's who you'll find calling Kandivali West home.

Kandivali West draws a remarkably diverse but well-settled population. Thakur Village is home to Gujarati business families, IT professionals and young couples who value the township's self-contained lifestyle. Lokhandwala Township attracts affluent professionals and senior executives. Charkop is dominated by government employees and Maharashtrian families who have lived there for decades. Poisar and Samata Nagar attract middle-income families seeking affordable housing with good connectivity.

Western Railway - Kandivali Station

Fast, slow and semi-fast trains to Churchgate (40 min) and Virar (25 min). Kandivali Station's west-side exit connects directly to S.V. Road and auto rickshaw stands.

Metro Line 2A - Dahisar to DN Nagar

Metro 2A has two stations serving Kandivali West - Kandivali and Akurli - connecting directly to Borivali, Malad, Goregaon and Andheri without road congestion.

BEST Bus Routes from Kandivali West

Key routes: 251, 252 (Thakur Village–Kandivali Station), 255 (Charkop–Borivali), 294 (Kandivali–Goregaon), 460 (Kandivali–Malad). Covers all internal pockets well.

S.V. Road & WEH Access from Kandivali West

S.V. Road runs through Kandivali West connecting to Borivali and Malad. The Western Express Highway is accessible via Akurli Road - ideal for BKC and airport commuters.
