About the Area

Versova, Mumbai - Beach, Koliwada & Metro Terminus

Versova is a coastal pocket of Andheri West's seafront - famous for Versova Beach and its landmark cleanup, the centuries-old Koliwada fishing village, the Madh ferry jetty and the western terminus of Mumbai Metro Line 1. It blends a laid-back seaside vibe with a strong film-industry presence on Yari Road.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Versova Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Versova has a relaxed, bohemian seaside soul. Mornings begin with fishing boats heading out and joggers on the cleaned-up beach, afternoons are quiet in the leafy Seven Bungalows and Aram Nagar lanes, and evenings come alive with Yari Road cafés, sunset crowds and a creative, easy-going energy. It's coastal Mumbai at its most laid-back.

Home to the traditional Koli fishing community, a large film-and-music fraternity along Yari Road, artists in Aram Nagar and well-settled families in Seven Bungalows, Versova blends old fishing-village heritage with new-age creative living - a mix unlike anywhere else in the city.
