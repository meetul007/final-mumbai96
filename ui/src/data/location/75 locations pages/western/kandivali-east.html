About the Area

Kandivali East, Mumbai - Family Township & Green Living Hub

Kandivali East is one of Mumbai's most sought-after residential destinations, anchored by the sprawling Thakur Village and Lokhandwala Township - two of the city's largest planned residential complexes. Character & Vibe

What Makes Kandivali East Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Kandivali East is quintessentially family Mumbai. It wakes up with school runs and morning walks in Thakur Village's parks, hits its stride by 9am as residents commute to BKC, Andheri and Lower Parel, and winds down gently by evening with families at the local sabzi mandi and kids at tuition classes. It's not a party suburb - it's a living suburb.

The Gujarati community is the backbone here - multi-generational families, strong vegetarian food culture, active community associations and a deep sense of neighbourhood pride. But Kandivali East is also home to Marathi families, South Indians, Punjabis and a growing number of young professionals drawn by relatively affordable housing and excellent connectivity via Metro Line 7.
