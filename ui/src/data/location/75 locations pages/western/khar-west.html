Khar West, Mumbai - Cafés, Lifestyle & Celebrity Living

Character & Vibe

What Makes Khar West Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Khar West has an effortlessly chic, lived-in glamour. Mornings are relaxed in the leafy numbered roads with joggers and café-goers, afternoons buzz on 16th Road and Linking Road, and evenings come alive with stylish dining and nightlife. Home to film and media personalities, fashion and creative professionals, well-settled families, a strong East Indian and Catholic presence, and the traditional Khar Danda Koli community, Khar West blends old Bombay heritage with new-age lifestyle living - one of the city's most aspirational addresses.
