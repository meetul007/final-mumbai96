About the Area

Andheri West, Mumbai - Lifestyle, Beach & Entertainment Hub

Andheri West is one of Mumbai's most vibrant and upscale western suburbs - home to Versova Beach, the leafy Lokhandwala Complex, buzzing markets at Four Bungalows and DN Nagar, and Yari Road's film-industry community. It blends premium residential living with shopping, dining and a thriving nightlife.

What Makes Andheri West Tick
