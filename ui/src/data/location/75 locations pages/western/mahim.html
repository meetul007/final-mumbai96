About the Area

Mahim, Mumbai - Where the Island City Meets the Suburbs

Mahim is one of Mumbai's oldest and most characterful neighbourhoods, sitting at the historic boundary between the island city and the western suburbs. It is home to the revered Mahim Dargah and St. Michael's Church, the old Koli fishing village of Mahim Koliwada, the seafront Mahim Beach and Fort, and the busy Mahim Junction linking three rail lines.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Mahim Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Mahim runs on heritage and the sea. Dawn belongs to the Koli fisherfolk heading out from Koliwada; mornings hum with the Dargah's devotees and Mori Road's markets; and evenings draw families to the breezy seafront along Cadell Road. It's an old, rooted Mumbai that has quietly held on to its identity even as Bandra and BKC boomed next door.

The community is richly mixed - Koli fishing families, Muslim residents around the Dargah, East Indian Catholics near the church, and old Maharashtrian and Gujarati households. That deep diversity, layered with centuries of history, gives Mahim a soulful, authentic Bombay character.
