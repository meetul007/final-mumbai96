What Makes Pali Hill Tick

Pali Hill moves to its own unhurried rhythm. Mornings bring joggers and dog-walkers along its shaded lanes; days are quiet and leafy behind high garden walls; and evenings glow softly as Pali Naka's cafes and bars fill with Bandra's creative set. It is the rare Mumbai address that feels both glamorous and genuinely peaceful.

The crowd is exclusive but eclectic - film stars, directors, fashion designers, business families and old Bandra residents who have called the hill home for decades. There's an understated confidence here: serious wealth worn lightly, wrapped in greenery and good taste.
