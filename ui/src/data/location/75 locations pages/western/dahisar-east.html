About the Area

Dahisar East, Mumbai - The City's Northeastern Gateway Suburb

Dahisar East is the northeastern gateway of Mumbai city, falling under the BMC's R/North ward. Anchored by the Dahisar toll naka on the Western Express Highway and well-served by Metro Line 7, it offers affordable city-limit living with excellent road, rail and metro connectivity, plus the green backdrop of Sanjay Gandhi National Park.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Dahisar East Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Dahisar East is a classic Mumbai middle-class suburb with a gateway edge. Mornings begin with the rush to the station, metro and highway, the local markets hum through the day, and evenings bring families out for street food and walks. It's value-driven Mumbai-city living - genuine 400-pin-code Mumbai at far gentler prices than the southern suburbs.

The community is a steady mix of Gujarati, Marathi and North Indian families, many settled here for decades. With Sanjay Gandhi National Park on its doorstep, the Metro 7 line and direct highway access, Dahisar East blends old-suburb warmth with a fast-improving quality of life.
