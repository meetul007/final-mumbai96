About the Area

Uttan, Mira-Bhayandar - Coastal Villages, Beaches & Weekend Getaways

Uttan is a scenic coastal belt at the northwestern tip of the Mira-Bhayandar Municipal Corporation (MBMC). Made up of traditional Koli fishing villages and East Indian Catholic hamlets, it is known for its beaches, fishing harbours, beach resorts, churches and a calm, semi-rural character that feels worlds away from city Mumbai.

Character & Vibe

What Makes Uttan Tick

An honest snapshot - the energy, the people, the pace. The kind of thing no other directory tells you.

Uttan moves to the rhythm of the sea, not the local train. Mornings begin with fishing boats returning to the harbour, the day passes slowly across villages and beaches, and weekends bring city visitors looking for a quick coastal escape. It's the most laid-back, semi-rural pocket of Mira-Bhayandar.

The community is deeply rooted - generations of Koli fisherfolk and East Indian Catholic families give Uttan a distinct cultural identity built around the sea, the church and the harvest of the coast. It feels like a village holding its ground at the edge of a fast-growing city.

Uttan is a coastal fishing-village belt at the northwestern tip of the Mira-Bhayandar Municipal Corporation. Known for its beaches, Koli and East Indian communities, resorts and churches, it offers a rare slice of calm, seaside living near Mumbai. Mumbai96 is here to connect this close-knit community and its weekend visitors with the local services they need.
